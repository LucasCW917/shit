Why Did Hitler Do Nothing Wrong?

There are many controversial figures throughout history. There are also people who are almost universally regarded as having done some extremely bad things. Adolf Hitler belongs rather firmly in the latter category.

Nevertheless, I have decided that everyone else is wrong.

Argument #1: He Was a Vegetarian

My first piece of evidence is that Hitler was a vegetarian.

This proves absolutely nothing.

In fact, after approximately fourteen seconds of research, I have discovered that being vegetarian does not make someone morally good. Therefore, my first argument has collapsed.

Argument #2: He Was Good at Public Speaking

Hitler was, objectively, capable of giving persuasive speeches. Unfortunately, being persuasive and being correct are two entirely different things. My second argument has therefore also collapsed.

Argument #3: He Painted

This one is particularly strong because I like paintings.

Unfortunately, several billion other people also painted things without becoming dictators responsible for mass murder, so this evidence is once again useless.

At this point, the reader may be wondering whether I have any actual evidence.

I do not.

This is shit.

An Extremely Serious Analysis of Shit

Shit.

There are few words in the English language that possess such extraordinary versatility. It can be a noun, a verb, an adjective, an exclamation, and, depending on how creative you are feeling, apparently an entire academic discipline.

Humanity has been producing shit for thousands of years. Despite this, society has somehow failed to develop a universally accepted philosophical framework for understanding it.

This is unacceptable.

What Is Shit?

Shit is generally understood to be the solid waste produced by the digestive system. After food enters the human body, the body extracts useful nutrients and sends the leftovers on a fascinating journey toward the toilet.

The final product is shit.

It is brown.

Usually.

Sometimes it is green.

This raises questions.

The Cultural Significance of Shit

Shit has appeared throughout human history, from ancient civilizations to modern bathrooms.

Ancient humans probably looked at shit and thought:

"I should probably not step in that."

This was an important early achievement in human civilization.

Modern humans have improved considerably. We now have toilets, plumbing, sewage treatment plants, toilet paper, air fresheners, and extremely complicated public restroom hand dryers.

Despite all these technological advancements, the fundamental process remains unchanged:

Eat → Digest → Shit → Flush

Humanity has reached the moon, developed artificial intelligence, and built computers capable of running video games at hundreds of frames per second.

And yet, at the end of the day, we still shit.

Perhaps this is the great equalizer.

The Philosophy of Shit

A philosopher might ask:

"If a tree falls in the forest and nobody hears it, does it make a sound?"

I ask:

"If you shit in a forest and nobody finds it, did you commit littering?"

I have no answer.

This is why I am not a philosopher.

Conclusion
